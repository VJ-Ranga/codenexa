PROJECT FAQ

Before we get started.

What exactly does CodeNexa do?

We're a digital agency that handles your entire online presence: we build fast, modern websites (including online stores), design your brand identity (logo, colors, style guide), and manage your social media (posts, reels, and ad campaigns). You can pick one service or combine all three.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on the package — a typical Essential site is ready in about 1–2 weeks, while Premium and Ultimate projects with eCommerce or custom features take longer. After our discovery call, we'll give you a clear timeline in writing before any work begins.

What's included in the 1 year of free support?

Every web package includes free hosting, an SSL certificate, and maintenance for the first full year — so your site stays online, secure, and up to date at no extra cost.

Do you only work with businesses in Sri Lanka?

We're based in Sri Lanka, but we work with clients anywhere. All communication happens over email and WhatsApp, and we keep you updated at every step of the process.

How do we get started?

Send us a message by email or WhatsApp and tell us about your project. We’ll discuss the scope, price, and timing with you before any work begins.
